FeedPlayer 隐私政策

责任边界

FeedPlayer 不运营用户账号、云媒体库，也不运营用于上传、存储或分析用户媒体内容的开发者服务器。你选择的媒体由设备直接访问，媒体内容不会经过 FeedPlayer 开发者的服务器。

1. 设备本地处理

为了浏览和播放媒体，FeedPlayer 会在设备上处理你选择的文件、文件信息和媒体内容。播放记录、缓存、缩略图、界面设置以及广告展示计时主要保存在设备本地。

SMB 服务器地址、端口、用户名、域和收藏目录等连接配置保存在设备上。如果你选择保存 SMB 密码，密码由 iOS Keychain 等系统安全机制保存，并仅用于连接你指定的服务器。

2. 你选择的文件服务

SMB 服务器、iCloud Drive 和其他文件提供者由你自行选择、配置和控制。FeedPlayer 开发者不运营这些服务。应用在你的设备与所选服务之间读取媒体，相关媒体内容不会先上传到或经过开发者服务器。

这些服务对数据的保存、传输和删除方式由相应服务的设置与隐私政策决定。

3. 广告与隐私选择

如果你尚未购买永久去广告，应用可能使用 Google AdMob 展示广告，并使用 Google User Messaging Platform（UMP）提供广告隐私选择。Google AdMob 和 UMP 是独立第三方服务。

设备或应用信息、IP 地址、广告互动、诊断信息以及广告隐私选择，可能由 Google 及其广告合作伙伴根据其自身隐私政策处理。FeedPlayer 开发者不会通过自有服务器主动收集或存储这些广告数据。

在适用地区，你可以通过应用显示的同意界面或“广告隐私选项”查看和更改选择。购买永久去广告后，FeedPlayer 将停止发起应用内广告请求。

Google 隐私权政策 · Google 如何使用合作伙伴应用中的信息

4. App 内购买

App 内购买和付款由 Apple StoreKit / App Store 处理。FeedPlayer 只读取商品信息和经 Apple 验证的购买状态，不会获得你的完整支付卡信息。

Apple 隐私政策

5. 删除与管理

你可以在 FeedPlayer 中清除缓存、播放记录、收藏和 SMB 配置，也可以通过 iOS/iPadOS 删除应用及其本地数据。清除 FeedPlayer 的本地数据不会删除 SMB、iCloud Drive 或其他文件服务中的原始媒体。

由 Google、Apple 或你选择的文件服务处理的数据，应通过相应第三方提供的设置、隐私工具或联系渠道进行管理或删除。FeedPlayer 开发者无法删除其并未持有的数据。

6. 联系方式与更新

如有隐私问题，请联系 xie181214@gmail.com。如果 FeedPlayer 的数据处理方式发生实质变化，本页面会同步更新。

FeedPlayer Privacy Policy

Responsibility boundary

FeedPlayer does not operate user accounts, a cloud media library, or developer servers for uploading, storing, or analyzing user media. Media you select is accessed directly by your device and does not pass through servers operated by the FeedPlayer developer.

1. On-device processing

To browse and play media, FeedPlayer processes the files, file information, and media content you select on your device. Playback history, caches, thumbnails, interface settings, and ad-display timing are primarily stored locally on the device.

SMB connection settings such as server address, port, username, domain, and favorite folders are stored on the device. If you choose to save an SMB password, it is stored using iOS security mechanisms such as Keychain and is used only to connect to the server you specify.

2. File services you choose

You choose, configure, and control any SMB server, iCloud Drive account, or other file provider. The FeedPlayer developer does not operate these services. The app reads media between your device and the selected service; that media is not first uploaded to or routed through developer servers.

How those services store, transfer, or delete data is governed by their settings and privacy policies.

3. Advertising and privacy choices

If you have not purchased permanent ad removal, the app may use Google AdMob to show ads and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to provide advertising privacy choices. Google AdMob and UMP are independent third-party services.

Device or app information, IP address, ad interactions, diagnostic information, and advertising privacy choices may be processed by Google and its advertising partners under their own privacy policies. The FeedPlayer developer does not actively collect or store this advertising data on developer-operated servers.

Where applicable, you can review or change your choices through the consent screen or Advertising Privacy Options shown by the app. After permanent ad removal is purchased, FeedPlayer stops making in-app advertising requests.

Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from partner apps

4. In-app purchases

In-app purchases and payments are processed by Apple StoreKit / App Store. FeedPlayer reads product information and purchase status verified by Apple, but does not receive your full payment-card information.

Apple Privacy Policy

5. Deletion and management

You can clear caches, playback history, favorites, and SMB configurations in FeedPlayer. You can also use iOS/iPadOS to delete the app and its local data. Clearing FeedPlayer data does not delete original media stored on SMB, iCloud Drive, or another file service.

Data processed by Google, Apple, or a file service you choose must be managed or deleted through that third party’s settings, privacy tools, or contact channels. The FeedPlayer developer cannot delete data the developer does not hold.

6. Contact and updates

For privacy questions, contact xie181214@gmail.com. This page will be updated if FeedPlayer’s data handling changes materially.
